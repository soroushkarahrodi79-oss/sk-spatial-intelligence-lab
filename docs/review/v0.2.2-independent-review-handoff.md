## Task A — independent domain review of all 52 entries

A suitable reviewer understands geospatial change analysis, topology, data provenance, and the distinction between reported geometry and geographic, temporal, legal or causal inference. The reviewer must not have authored this vocabulary, its deterministic gate, generator, or original labels. Independently inspect the upstream GeoImpact-CI V1.2.0 contractual sources, not merely the text excerpts in the packet.

For each stable entry ID, determine **Approve, Reject, or Change** and document: (i) why the statement is justified or overstated, (ii) conditional applicability, (iii) exact source field binding and unit, (iv) negations and abstentions, and (v) any unresolved concern. Pay particular attention to A01–A06 and A12, and to abstention sentences that contain substantive affirmative statements. The 26 grouped ambiguity variants still require 26 explicit decisions.

Declare in writing your role and relationship to the author, the date of actual review, whether source contracts were consulted, and any conflicts of interest. A name in a JSON file alone cannot prove reviewer identity or independence.

Only **after** a human actually completes and signs the decisions may they be transcribed into `geoimpact-copilot/semantic/review-record.json` with the matching digest and version. A Reject or Change keeps the review gate on HOLD. No AI agent should impersonate or fill the human decision fields.

## Task B — independent review of the 18 expected-answer labels

Examine the underlying report contracts, fixture content, exact expected values/bindings and abstention rationale **without using model predictions as an oracle**. The reviewer should be independent of the person who wrote the labels. Record disagreement, necessary correction, confidence and reviewer attribution. If substantive labels or source vocabulary change, invalidate the affected previous review/digest, regenerate the packet as specified, and review the new material again. The current 18 fixture labels cannot substitute for a genuine independent test set.

## Submission and privacy

Request the finished decision sheet and written reasoning via a private channel chosen by the reviewer; do not publish personal contact details, signatures, private inputs or credentials. Submit a name and date **only with the person's actual consent and participation**. Retain the packet's digest, reviewed entry IDs, disagreement record and the evidence URLs examined.

Review artifacts are not proof of verified real-world reports. Real report selection, licence validation, and test-set sealing are separate gates.

## Do-not-cross gates

- No automatic `reviewed` status, invented human decision, or fabricated independence.
- No model calls, SDK, secrets, deployment, or changes to GeoImpact-CI, Madrid Lens or Salmas Flood.
- No benchmark report fabricated or relabelled as a real report.
- Do not send third-party-provider data unless each source's rights have been independently cleared.
