How evidence is produced

From source to interpretation

A single, inspectable path turns raw inputs into bounded conclusions. Each stage is reproducible and leaves a record.

01

Source

Identify authoritative data with its provenance, licence, geographic scope and acquisition time.

02

Validate

Check structure, coordinate reference system, geometry validity, units and cross-field consistency before use.

03

Compute

Run deterministic, inspectable spatial operations with fixed, reproducible parameters.

04

Evaluate

Compare outputs against trusted references and record error, uncertainty and reproducibility.

05

Interpret

State conclusions with their scope, uncertainty and limits — and what they do not support.

Generative AI may describe or request computations; it does not create authoritative spatial measurements. Deterministic software computes every number and geometry.
